We are always looking for motivated and curious students who are interested in working with us on theses, research projects, or internships.

If you are excited about topics such as machine learning, medical image analysis, or trustworthy AI, we would be happy to hear from you. Students at all levels are welcome to reach out and explore possible opportunities within our group.

Below you can find a list of currently available topics. However, if none of these directly match your interests, please do not hesitate to contact us anyway. We strongly encourage initiative applications and are always open to discussing your own ideas or project proposals.

We look forward to hearing from you!

## Open

If you are interested in pursuing a thesis, contact me at [d.wulff@uni-rostock.de](mailto:d.wulff@uni-rostock.de).

If there are no open topics available that fit your knowledge or interests, please feel free to reach out nonetheless. If capacity allows, we are happy to discuss potential thesis ideas together or explore how your own interests can be developed into a suitable research topic.


| Title | Level | Description | Requirements |
|------|------|------------|--------------|
| Integration of Anatomical Priors into Neural Networks for Organ Segmentation | Master | The project investigates the learning of probabilistic anatomical distributions (shape, size, and spatial relationships) from medical imaging data and their integration as priors into neural networks. These priors are leveraged to enhance segmentation performance under limited field-of-view and domain shifts. | Python, Deep Learning, Image Processing |
| Multi-Task Representation Learning for Vascular Morphology and Pathology | Master | This project aims to learn meaningful representations of vascular segments that capture both vessel morphology and pathological characteristics, such as the presence of stenosis. To achieve this, a multi-task learning framework will be developed, combining tasks such as vessel segmentation, stenosis detection, image reconstruction, and contrastive learning of healthy and diseased vessels. | Python, Deep Representation Learning, Image Processing |
| Development and Validation of an AI-Assisted 3D Ultrasound Image Annotation Tool | Master/ Bachelor | This project aims to develop and validate an AI-assisted annotation tool for 3D ultrasound volumes, potentially building on existing open-source software. The tool will integrate foundation models for automated segmentation to substantially reduce manual annotation effort, enabling medical experts to focus primarily on reviewing and correcting the generated segmentations. Validation will assess the tool’s segmentation performance and its potential to improve annotation efficiency. | Python, Deep Learning, Foundation Models, Image Processing |
